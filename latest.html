💰 Rebalancing Bands: Let Drift Trigger the Decision

+

Financial Health1 August 2026Portfolio discipline

+ +
+
🧒 Like you're 5
+

Imagine carrying two shopping bags. You planned to put half the weight in each, but one bag keeps getting heavier. Instead of guessing every day, you make a rule: move items only when one bag becomes clearly too heavy.

+

Your investments drift the same way. Rebalancing bands turn “Should I buy or sell now?” into a pre-written rule, so market excitement and fear do not get the final vote.

+
+ +
↓ Now call it what it really is: restoring target risk after allocation drift ↓
+ +
+

🔧 For the engineer

+
drift = current allocation − target allocation
rebalance when |drift| ≥ chosen band
+
+
Set target allocationDefine percentages from goal horizon, risk capacity, and need for liquidity—not recent returns. Example: 70% equity, 30% debt.
+
Choose one triggerUse an absolute band such as ±5 percentage points. A 70% equity target triggers below 65% or above 75%. Write rule before volatility arrives.
+
Prefer cash-flow rebalancingDirect new SIPs, bonuses, or dividends toward underweight assets first. This reduces selling, taxes, exit loads, and unnecessary transactions.
+
Review, do not constantly tradeCheck quarterly or half-yearly. Bands decide whether action is needed; calendar only schedules inspection.
+
Measure at portfolio levelCombine EPF, debt funds, equity mutual funds, and direct stocks by asset class. Rebalancing one account in isolation can hide total exposure.
+
Respect frictionBefore selling, check capital-gains tax, exit load, lock-in, and transaction cost. Restore risk with least-cost route, not perfect arithmetic.
+
+

Example: A ₹10 lakh portfolio targets 70:30. Equity rises to ₹8 lakh while debt stays ₹3 lakh, making equity 72.7%. With a ±5-point band, no trade: keep directing contributions to debt. At 76%, rebalance toward 70% using new cash first, then sell only if needed.

+
+ +
+
⚡ Micro-action (5 minutes)
+

Open your portfolio tracker. Write one target split and one absolute band beside it—for example, Equity 70%, rebalance outside 65–75%. Add a quarterly reminder to measure total allocation across every account.
