✨ Day 4
SRE is like a tightrope walker who builds their own safety net. You're responsible for system reliability, but your real skill is in designing systems that catch themselves when they stumble. Google's SRE philosophy centers on Error Budgets - the acceptable amount of downtime. When you exceed it, all feature work stops and 100% of effort goes to reliability. This creates powerful incentives to build robust systems from the start. This directly applies to our work on NVCF - every reliability improvement we make extends our error budget, giving us more room for innovation. The technical trio: SLOs (Service Level Objectives) define success, Error Budgets measure our risk allowance, and Postmortems capture lessons when we fail. Together, they form a feedback loop of continuous improvement.
⚡ Today's micro-action: Identify one service where improving monitoring could recover 1+ hour of error budget weekly.
