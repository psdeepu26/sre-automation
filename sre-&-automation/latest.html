💰 Goal Buckets: Match Risk to When You Need the Money

+

Financial Health8 August 2026Goal-based investing

+ +
+
🧒 Like you're 5
+

Imagine packing for three trips: one tomorrow, one next year, and one ten years away. You keep tomorrow's ticket in your pocket, next year's supplies in a cupboard, and spend time growing food for the distant trip.

+

Money works the same way. Cash needed soon should not ride a roller coaster. Money needed much later can tolerate bumps because it has time to recover.

+
+ +
↓ Now call it what it really is: matching asset risk to each goal's time horizon ↓
+ +
+

🔧 For the engineer

+
risk capacity ≠ risk appetite
risk capacity rises when time-to-goal, flexibility, and surplus cash rise
+
+
Start with liabilities, not productsDefine each goal as amount, due date, priority, and flexibility. “₹20 lakh in 8 years” is actionable; “invest for the future” is not.
+
Near bucket: 0–3 yearsOptimize for capital availability and low volatility. Emergency fund and committed near-term expenses belong here; chasing extra return can create forced selling.
+
Middle bucket: 3–7 yearsUse a controlled mix based on how flexible the date and amount are. Reduce equity exposure as the deadline approaches instead of waiting for the final year.
+
Far bucket: 7+ yearsDiversified equity can carry more weight because recovery time is longer. Allocation still depends on loss tolerance, income stability, and goal importance.
+
Use a glide pathMove money toward lower-volatility assets as time-to-goal shrinks. This converts accumulated gains into deadline certainty and limits sequence risk near withdrawal.
+
Track funded ratioCompare current goal assets with required present value. Review allocation and contribution rate separately; poor funding is not always solved by taking more risk.
+
+

Example: A home down payment due in two years and retirement due in fifteen years should not share one allocation. The home bucket prioritizes availability; the retirement bucket can accept more volatility. Same person, different goals, different risk capacity.

+

⚡ Micro-action (5 minutes)
+

Pick one financial goal. Write its amount, due date, years remaining, and whether the date can move. Label it near, middle, or far, then check whether its current asset mix matches that horizon.
