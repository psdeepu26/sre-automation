Retry Storm Control

🧒 Like you're 5

A shop temporarily closes its door. If every customer knocks again every second, the shopkeeper cannot reopen—the knocking becomes the new problem.

Better customers wait longer after each failed knock, choose slightly different wait times, and eventually stop. The shop gets quiet space to recover.

↓ Now let’s call it what it really is ↓

🔧 For the engineer

Retries convert transient failures into success—but during overload they multiply demand. If original request rate is R and each request makes k extra attempts, downstream load approaches R × (1 + k). Recovery slows, timeouts grow, and synchronized clients can create a positive feedback loop.

Exponential backoff

Increase delay after each failure: base × 2attempt, capped at a maximum. This reduces pressure while dependency recovers.

Full jitter

Randomize each wait: random(0, capped backoff). This breaks client synchronization and smooths retry bursts.

Retry budget

Cap retries as a fraction of successful traffic or total calls. Example: retry traffic must remain below 10% of primary attempts.

Deadline awareness

Retry only when remaining request deadline can cover backoff plus another attempt. Otherwise fail fast instead of doing doomed work.

Error classification

Retry transient failures such as selected 429, 502, or 503. Do not retry validation errors or non-idempotent operations without safeguards.

Server guidance

Honor Retry-After. Pair client limits with server admission control, circuit breakers, and load shedding.

# Policy sketch
max_attempts: 3
per_try_timeout: 800ms
total_deadline: 2500ms
backoff:
  strategy: exponential
  base: 100ms
  max: 1000ms
  jitter: full
retry_on:
  - connect_failure
  - 429
  - 502
  - 503
budget:
  max_retry_ratio: 0.10

Observe the mechanism: graph retry rate separately from request rate. Alert on retry_attempts / primary_attempts, exhausted retry budgets, and success-after-retry. A falling error rate with rising retry amplification can still mean the system is near collapse.

Design rule: one layer should own retries. Application, sidecar, gateway, and SDK retries can multiply each other invisibly.

⚡ Micro-action (5 minutes)

Pick one production client. Write down its max attempts, backoff, jitter, retryable errors, total deadline, and retry owner. If any answer is unknown—or multiple layers retry—create one concrete follow-up item.
